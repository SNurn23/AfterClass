About

Our goal is to help find or form study groups so that students, teachers or anyone who needs it is not a challenge.

You can create your own study groups
    Steps:
  • 1 - Go to "Groups" section.
  • 2 - Click on the "Add Group" button.
  • 3 - Complete the form with the requested data.
  • 4 - Click on "Save"
  • 5 - Done! Your group will appear in your profile in "My Groups" section.
You can join groups
    Steps:
  • 1- Go to "Groups".
  • 2- Select a group of your choice.
  • 3- Then press "Enter".
  • 4- Done! You are now part of the group.
  • 5- You can check it in the "My groups" section.
You can create posts and meetings
    Steps:
  • Go to the group profile.

  • POST

  • 1 - Go to the "Post" section.
  • 2 - You can create a post with the "Add Post" button and also delete it with the "Delete" button.
  • 3 - You can see all the posts that have been made

  • MEETINGS

  • 1 - You can create a Meeting and also delete or edit it.
  • 2 - You can see all the meetings that have been created.
